Panning with the middle button does nothing

Use space + drag with the left button instead (it does exactly the same job).

The utility that came with your mouse may be turning the middle button into some other job. When the wheel zooms but pressing it down does nothing, suspect that first.

The text is too small to read

Text here is at full size. Text of height 5 is supposed to look small when you view the whole drawing. Go closer and you can read it.

Its size on paper can also be tidied with the multiplier in the print preview.

Dashed lines look solid

The spacing does not suit the drawing. Type LTS 自動 (auto) → Enter.

I cannot select it, or delete it

The layer that object is on may be locked or hidden.

Open the layer panel with LA and check the padlock and the eye.

I changed the layer colour but the object did not change colour

That object carries a colour of its own. Select it and use SETBYLAYER = the tag is stripped off and it follows the layer.

I made a mistake

U goes back, as many times as you like. Trims, deletions and arrays all come back.

A DWG will not open

Asking the sender for a DXF is the sure way (every CAD program can write one). On the Mac version, typing brew install libredwg in Terminal brings in the translator and they will open.

A PDF will not open

  • A scanned (image) PDF has no line coordinates and cannot be brought in.
  • A PDF whose author forbade copying, and a PDF with a password, are deliberately not opened: we respect the rights and the lock.
