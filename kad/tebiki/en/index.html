K'Ad Illustrated Manual

Click the name of a task and you get just those steps. Open this page in a second tab or a second window beside K'Ad, and follow it while you draw.

You can also print it and keep it on your desk (your browser's Print gives you the whole chapter).

This handbook is baked from the manual of the version still being built. It differs from the version now being distributed (Web: kiln 17-23, desktop: 4.0.0) in two places. (1) When the drawing goes beyond the paper frame: in the distributed version no card appears at the top right, and the paper follows the centre of the drawing. (2) The button names in the 3D room: in the distributed version they are "複写 / 回す / サイズ" (in this handbook, "複製 / 回転 / 拡大縮小").
